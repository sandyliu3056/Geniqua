Carrier

What will the carrier actually bill?

Parcel carriers charge on whichever is greater: the weight on the scale, or the space the box takes on the truck.

The divisor is set by your contract, not by us. Check your carrier agreement before you rely on a number here.

Will this box trigger a surcharge?

Enter the box in inches and pounds. We compare it against the published US ground thresholds and show which ones it crosses.

Thresholds are the carriers' published US ground values and change most years. Packaging-based handling charges and over-maximum charges depend on what the carrier records at the hub, so treat those as a heads-up rather than a verdict.
